Financial transparency

Donors deserve to know exactly what their money does, and deserve to be told when a document is not ready yet. This page holds our registration details, our safeguarding commitment, and the reports we publish.

Legal status

Feiyah Action Network is registered as a community based organisation in Kenya. Rose Orguba founded FAN in 2008 and registered it in 2010. It is based in Laisamis, Marsabit County, and works with Samburu and Rendille communities across the constituency. Registration documents are available on request.

Safeguarding

FAN works directly with girls and young people. Staff and volunteers follow a child and adult safeguarding policy when running programs, and FAN treats that as a condition of the work rather than an afterthought. The policy will be published on this page.

Annual reports and budget summaries

FAN has not yet published an annual report or budget summary. We would rather say that plainly than point you at a document that does not exist. When you ask for figures, we will send what we have. Write to info@feiyahactionnetwork.org or use the contact form.

Who funds the work

FAN runs the Komesha FGM Sasa campaign in partnership with ActionAid and UNFPA. Beyond that, the organisation is supported by direct contributions from individuals.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, ask us. Accountability to the communities we serve and to the people who fund the work is part of the work.
